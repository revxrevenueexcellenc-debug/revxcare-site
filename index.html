    Discovery Call

    We start by learning how your practice works. On the call, we review what you do, who you bill, and what's going wrong. Then we show you where you're losing money and how we'd stop it.

    Clear scope. Clear investment. Clear outcomes. You receive a written plan covering services, deliverables, timelines, and transparent pricing built around your practice size and goals. Everything is agreed in writing before work begins.

    Protected from day one. Service agreement and BAA are signed digitally before any patient information is shared, so your practice is HIPAA-compliant from the very first exchange.

    Your data, safely handled. We set up encrypted, access-controlled channels for your documents and PHI, and align with your EHR or practice management system. Every access is logged and every document is tracked.

  6. 6

    Quality, Reporting

    Performance you can see. Every deliverable goes through quality review. Scheduled reports and regular check-ins keep you informed, and we keep refining the process as your practice grows.
